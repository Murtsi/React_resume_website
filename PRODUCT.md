# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical recruiters, engineering managers, collaborators, and peers who want a quick but credible read on Antti Murtokangas' practical engineering profile.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Antti's applied work across AI systems, backend engineering, automation, and security research. It should make the reader understand what he builds, what technical direction interests him, and where there is real proof of work.

## Brand Personality

Technical, careful, research-minded.

## Anti-references

The site should not feel like a generic personal landing page, a loud hacker persona, or a decorative SaaS template. Security content should avoid naming sensitive targets and should emphasize responsible disclosure.

## Design Principles

- Lead with concrete shipped or researched work instead of broad claims.
- Keep the interface calm, compact, and technical.
- Make AI security and AppSec interest visible without overstating experience.
- Treat security research as responsible disclosure, not spectacle.
- Preserve readability and fast scanning on both desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site should remain keyboard-readable, responsive, and high contrast. Motion should stay minimal and should not block content for users who prefer reduced motion.
